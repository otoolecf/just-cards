Cribbage

A 2 player game with a standard 52-card deck. Deal 6 cards into each player's hand. Each player contributes 2 cards to the dealer's crib. Keep the remaining stock available for cutting a starter card.

Players play cards to a shared board, counting a running total no higher than 31. Score pairs, runs and combinations totaling fifteen. After play, score each hand and then the dealer's crib. The first player to reach 121 points wins.
