Shithead

This shedding game is for 2 to 5 players. Give each player 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up cards and a private hand of 3 cards. Keep the rest in the stock and use a central discard pile.

Play one or more cards of the same rank onto the discard pile. Match or exceed the top rank, or pick up the pile if no legal play is possible. Draw from stock until the hand contains 3 cards while stock remains.

After emptying the hand, play the face-up cards and then the face-down cards. A two resets the pile and a ten clears it. The last player holding cards loses.
