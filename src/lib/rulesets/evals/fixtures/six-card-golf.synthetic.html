Six Card Golf

This test game supports 2 to 6 players and lasts 9 rounds. Lowest score wins.

Deal 6 cards to each player in a grid of 2 rows and 3 columns. Each player turns 2 cards face up. Put the remaining cards in a face-down stock pile and start a face-up discard pile.

On a turn, draw from the stock or discard pile. Replace one card in your grid and discard the replaced card, or discard the drawn card. Reveal the grid when the round ends.

Scoring: aces are worth 1 point; twos are worth -2 points; kings are worth 0 points; jacks and queens are worth 10 points. Other numbered cards use their face value. Two matching cards in a column cancel to zero.
